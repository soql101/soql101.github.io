Zenith ERP

Privacy policy · last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

Zenith ERP is software that a jewellery shop installs on its own computer. This policy explains what the software does with information, including information from a Google account the shop connects.

Where the shop's data is kept

Bills, stock, parties, ledgers and every other record are stored in a database on the shop's own computer. They are not sent to us or to any server we run. Backups go only to a folder the shop chooses.

Google account and Google Drive

A shop may connect its own Google account to share bills with a link. Zenith ERP asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates itself. It cannot see any other file in the shop's Drive.

  • What it does: when a person at the shop presses Share or Update on Drive, Zenith ERP creates or replaces the PDF of that one paper in a Zenith ERP folder in the shop's Drive, and sets it so anyone with the link can view it. It also reads the account's email address, to show which account is connected.
  • What it keeps: the file's link and identifier, stored in the shop's own database. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the shop's computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • What it never does: it does not read, list or delete other files, does not upload anything in the background, and does not send Google data to us or to anyone else. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • Links: anyone the shop sends a link to, and anyone they forward it to, can open that PDF. A link cannot be guessed, but it is not private.

Zenith ERP's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

WhatsApp

Zenith ERP does not send WhatsApp messages. It opens WhatsApp on the shop's computer with the customer's number and the message typed in, and a person at the shop decides whether to press Send. The app keeps a record that it opened WhatsApp, in the shop's own database.

Removing access

The shop can disconnect Google Drive in Settings › Google Drive, or remove Zenith ERP's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in Drive stay there until the shop deletes them.
