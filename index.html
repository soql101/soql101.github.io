Zenith ERP

Billing, stock and accounts for jewellery shops

Zenith ERP is desktop software for Indian jewellery shops. It is installed on the shop's own computer, and other counters in the shop connect to it over the shop's own network. The shop's books stay on that computer.

What it does

  • Sale and purchase bills, returns, quotations and vouchers, in two books.
  • Stock by weight and by tag, with tag printing.
  • Party ledgers in rupees and in gold and silver weight.
  • Cash book, bank book and reports, including GST returns.
  • Pawn (girvi) and savings schemes.
  • Sharing a bill on WhatsApp, with a link to its PDF kept in the shop's own Google Drive.

Google Drive

When a shop connects its own Google account, Zenith ERP puts the PDF of a bill the shop chooses to share into a Zenith ERP folder in that account's Drive, and nothing else. Read the privacy policy.
